How to use

Orbit the camera by dragging, zoom with the wheel. Tell the arm what to do in one sentence: pick up, grab, move, or place an object on the shelf or table. Use a color + shape, e.g. red cube, blue cylinder, yellow sphere. Say reset to park the arm.
